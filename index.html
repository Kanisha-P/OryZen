00 · the belief

A rice mill makes a mountain of husk every day. Most people see waste. We see raw material.

OryZen turns rice husk, an abundant and largely unmanaged farm by-product, into biodegradable packaging made right here in West Bengal.

01 · the problem

Two crises, one husk.

Rice residue gets burned. Single-use plastic gets dumped. Both are growing.

05 · questions

The things people actually ask.

Is OryZen tested yet?

It is a homemade prototype. Feasibility tests for water resistance, flexibility and biodegradability are planned. Heat resistance and leak-proofing are goals for later, not claims.

Why rice husk?

It is produced all year at every rice mill, makes up about a fifth of paddy weight, and has no large-scale commercial use in India yet.

Why not bagasse, straw or coir?

Those are often seasonal, already used elsewhere, or not readily available in West Bengal. Husk is.

Who is it for?

Local food vendors, sweet shops and eco-conscious retailers in Kolkata first, then wider.

What can it become?

Rigid trays, plates and tableware, or flexible bags and wraps, all from one formula.
